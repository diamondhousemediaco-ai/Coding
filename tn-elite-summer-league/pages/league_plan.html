Why This Exists

The Anti-AAU

Families are burned out on pay-to-play chaos: $3,000–$5,000 summers, five games a day in silent gyms, no development, no stakes. We flip every one of those. One venue. One draft. Real coaching. Every game streamed with names on the back. Unrivaled proved the formula — a $35M raise to a $340M valuation in 18 months on the strength of branding, scarcity, and player-first economics. OTE proved the content engine works for high-school-age hoops. Nobody has built it at the state level. We go first.

Top ~300 invited. Measurements, athletic testing, 5-on-5 evaluation. Combine fee waived for invitees. College-style content moment #1.

03

Draft Night

Produced draft at the Curb Event Center. Caps, cameras, families, sponsors. The single most clippable night of the year.

04

Two Seasons + Nationals

Two 5-week seasons with playoffs, then (Year 2–3) a 10-day national invitational — real programs from across the country.

The feeling — why this will play like college ball

Eighty of the best girls basketball players in Tennessee. Eighty of the best boys. One building. Every player in the gym earned the jersey through a tryout, a combine, and a draft — nobody bought a roster spot, so there are no passengers on the floor. When all ten players in a game are top-of-the-state talent, every screen gets fought over, every closeout is real, every possession is contested — the game plays at college speed because everyone on the floor forces it to. Add practices against the best, film like a program, standings that count, D2 whistles, an arena crowd, and a summer where the only way to shine is to beat somebody as good as you — and what you get isn't a summer league. It's college basketball, arriving two years early. Kids will leave better because the water they swam in all summer was deeper. That's the product. Everything else in this plan is just how we deliver it.

The player deal

One season fee — ~$400 all-in for the whole summer (tryout fee credited, combine free for invitees) vs. $3,000+ for an AAU summer. Uniforms, coaching, streaming, stats, and trainer coverage included. Endgame (Full Vision): $0 — fully sponsor-funded. And because TSSAA has allowed high-school NIL since Dec 2022 (no cap), sponsors can legally pay players for content and appearances — no school marks, no school-coach facilitation.

Compliance, Handled

Clean By Design

Nothing about this league touches school-team rules — and we keep it that way with four hard guardrails.

Independent league TSSAA

Players sign up as individuals, outside their school programs — exactly like AAU, which runs all summer. TSSAA's independent-team restriction applies only during the school season. Dead-period rules restrict school coaches and their own players; our draft rule guarantees that pairing never exists. We'll still get written TSSAA confirmation on file — diligence, not a blocker.

Amateurism & NIL TSSAA

Players pay a (small) fee, keeping amateur status simple. Free-gear and expense questions get a written TSSAA ruling before launch. NIL is legal in TN with no cap — sponsor-to-player deals run direct, no school marks, no coach facilitation.

NCAA event certification ECAG

We certify as a nonscholastic event so D-I coaches can attend during live periods: BBCS registration for all athletes/coaches, USA Basketball Gold Licenses (~$68/coach, background check included), $1M/$2M liability with NCAA as additional insured, athletic trainer at every venue. Total fees are trivial (~$430 first year) — it's the process that must be airtight.

Safety stack

Background checks + SafeSport-style training for every adult. Certified athletic trainers at every event day. This is the "legit" families are starving for — and it's a selling point, not a cost center.

College-level officiating No AAU refs

The single loudest AAU complaint is terrible officiating — so we don't hire AAU crews, and we skip past HS crews too. Three-man college crews (D2) work every game, recruited through the college officiating coordinators we already know. Summer is their offseason; a full schedule of games at one venue is an easy yes. Budgeted at $150/official/game ($450/crew — ~$70K season) vs the $35–75 AAU pays. Players get taught the college whistle before they ever get to campus, and parents feel the difference in the first quarter.

Coach quality bar

Head coaches are recruited like a staff, not volunteers: college assistants, former pros, and decorated HS coaches (never with their own school's players). Every team also gets an assistant, and a league-wide interim pool guarantees no team ever goes uncoached. Real practices, real scouting, real film — development is the product.

The Stage & The Stream

Built For Broadcast

"The highest stage before college" is a production claim, not a slogan. Every game streams from day one, and there's a deliberate ladder from our own channels to ESPN. Here's where games live, what real production costs, and how to make every night read big-league on camera.

LEVEL 1 · YEAR 1

Own Channels

League YouTube for full games + TikTok/IG/YT Shorts highlight engine. This is OTE's actual model — 23M views on opening weekend, all owned audience and data. Free distribution, and sponsors buy the clips.

LEVEL 2 · YEAR 1

League Pass

Summer pass ($10–15/mo) or per-season pass on our site. Parents, relatives, and college staffs subscribe — BallerTV built an entire business on exactly this. Revenue, plus emails of every fan.

LEVEL 3 · YEAR 2

Local TV

Nashville independent stations sell airtime — a weekly "Game of the Week" time-buy runs low four figures a night and instantly reprices every sponsor asset ("as seen on TV").

LEVEL 4 · YEAR 2–3

ESPN+ / National

High-school hoops reaches ESPN through packagers — Paragon Marketing has curated ESPN's HS slate since the LeBron game in 2002. ESPN airs only ~12 HS games a year, anchored on ESPN-100 recruits. The route in is star power + a broadcast-quality feed, not a check.

Room Nights = Leverage

Hotels & Housing

Youth sports is the hotel industry's favorite customer — predictable blocks, summer dates, families who eat out every meal. That gives us three plays, and Nationals turns them into serious money.

1 · Official Host Hotel

One flagship property near Belmont (Midtown/Music Row corridor) becomes "Official Hotel of the League": cash + trade deal — discounted staff/visiting-team rooms and comp suites in exchange for branding, in-arena signage, and first call on all league business. Trade value often beats cash at this tier.

2 · Room-Block Rebates

League-managed booking links ("Stay with the League") for every out-of-town family, with a $10–25 per room-night rebate paid back to the league — standard youth-sports housing economics. For Nationals, make it stay-to-play: invited teams must book through league housing to compete. 16 traveling teams × ~14 people × 9 nights ≈ 2,000+ room-nights before families and fans — worth $20–50K in rebates alone, plus real leverage on rates.

3 · The Tourism Machine

Room-night-generating events are exactly what the Nashville Sports Council and Visit Music City exist to fund — bid fees, marketing grants, and services for events that fill hotels. Nationals is a textbook application. This is free institutional money most youth operators never ask for.

Housing safety — non-negotiables

Minors living away from home is the highest-trust thing this league will ever do, so the rules are absolute: every adult with residence access is background-checked and SafeSport-trained · girls' and boys' floors fully separated with separate supervising staff · nightly check-in log · written parent consent + 24/7 league contact line · zero-tolerance policy in the parent handbook, published before tryouts. Done right, the Residence isn't a liability — it's the loudest possible proof that this league takes care of kids, and it's what unlocks statewide talent no Nashville-only operation could touch.
