March – May 2027 · Built Around The School Calendar

The Road In

Spring belongs to the schools — state tournaments, proms, AP exams, graduations, Spring Fling. So the preseason is engineered around all of it: tryouts start after the TSSAA basketball state tournaments, the combine wraps before AP exams begin, Draft Night lands the evening AP exams end and before graduation weekends, and seniors owe the league nothing until school is fully behind them.

The conflict map — what this calendar dodges

TSSAA basketball state tournaments (early–mid March): tryouts start Mar 20, after the brackets clear — and state-tourney players never get punished for a deep run. · Prom season (April Saturdays): tryouts end by 4 PM, evenings untouched. · AP exams (May 3–14): two-week league blackout; Draft Night lands the evening the window closes. · Graduation weekends (May 15–23): zero obligations — we celebrate grads instead of scheduling against them. · TSSAA Spring Fling (late May): auto-excused from camp. · July 4th: holiday week practices shift. · School restart (early August): Season 2 closes Aug 7–9, evenings only, before classrooms fill.
