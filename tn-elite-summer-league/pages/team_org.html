The design principle

Six owners, not sixty helpers. Every function in the league reports up through exactly one director, and every director owns a number: Basketball owns competition integrity, Media owns audience, Partnerships owns revenue, Events owns the night, Compliance owns zero-incidents, Finance owns the books. Coordinators and interns (Belmont · Lipscomb · MTSU · TSU sport-management and broadcasting programs) plug in under all six — a built-in talent pipeline that also makes the league part of Nashville's campus fabric.

Director of Basketball Operations

Full Vision: $90K full-time

Mission: Own the product on the floor. If the basketball isn't real — real competition, real officiating, real development — nothing else matters. This person makes the "highest stage before college" claim true.

First 90 Days (from Jan 2027)

  • Tryout registration live statewide; evaluation rubric locked
  • Rulebook finalized with the officiating coordinator (format, shot clock, bonus, point-diff cap)

KPIs

100% of games with full 3-man college crews · zero eligibility incidents · schedule runs on time all summer · 600 tryout registrations · every team fields 10 players both seasons

Profile

Former college assistant / DOBO, or a decorated HS coach with administrative chops. Organized enough to run a draft, respected enough to tell a head coach no.

Where to find them

Belmont/Lipscomb/TSU/Vanderbilt basketball ops alumni; TN college assistants between jobs; the D2 officiating coordinators you already know will also point you to the right ops people.

Basketball Staff

Head Coach ×16

$3,500/summer (Full Vision $8,000)
Reports to: Dir. Basketball Operations

Mission: Coach a drafted team like a college program for ten weeks — real practices, real film, real development — and send every kid back to their school better than they arrived.

Core Responsibilities

  • Run the weekly rhythm: Monday/Tuesday 90-minute practice, optional Wednesday film, two games a week — ~20 practices and 18–23 games across the summer
  • Build a per-player development plan at the draft; deliver a written development report to each family at season's end (the anti-AAU receipt)
  • Draft-night participation: build the board, make the picks, wear the brand
  • Uphold league standards: bench conduct with college officials, equal-development rotation floors in regular season (playoffs are earned minutes), media availability after marquee games
  • Hard rule: never rostered with players from your own school — enforced at the draft

Selection Criteria

  • College assistants and former pros first; decorated HS coaches welcome (cross-school only); skills trainers with team-coaching résumés considered
  • USA Basketball Gold License + background check + SafeSport training required before first practice

Why they say yes

Streamed games with commentary = a public coaching reel · college staffs courtside on live-period weekends · a real draft, real standings, real championship · paid, unlike most summer gigs.

Where to find them

TN college assistant ranks (all levels), pro/overseas alumni back home in Nashville-Memphis-Knoxville, and the state's best HS coaches looking to coach elite talent without recruiting headaches.

Assistant Coach ×16

$1,750/summer (FV $4,000)

One per team, hired with the head coach. Owns scouting the next opponent, running development stations in practice, and bench organization on game nights. Same licensing/screening stack as head coaches. The league's HC succession pipeline.

Interim Coach Pool 4–6 floaters

$150–250/game · league-wide

Fully licensed coaches with no team assignment who attend league nights ready to step in — illness, travel, ejection, emergency. No team ever goes uncoached; no game ever gets forfeited for a staffing gap. Between assignments they scout for the league's content and stats teams.

One honest note on Year 1 comp

These are believer salaries — seasonal money for people who want to build something. The offer that makes them fair: a written path to the Full Vision full-time role ("you build it, you run it at $85–120K when it scales"), a title they keep, and public credit on a property Nashville will be watching. Equity or bonus points for directors who hit their KPIs are worth considering — it's cheaper than cash and binds the core team to Year 2.
