# League Org / Staffing Plan — working notes

## Front office (year-round core, small)
- Commissioner / League President (founder) — vision, sponsors, media
- Director of Basketball Operations — combine, draft, schedules, officials, rosters
- Director of Partnerships & Revenue — sponsor sales, activation, renewals
- Director of Media & Content — streaming, social, brand (Unrivaled-style content engine)
- Director of Events & Logistics — venue, game-day ops, vendors, security
- Compliance & Player Welfare Coordinator — TSSAA/NCAA rules, eligibility, SafeSport, background checks
- Finance/Admin (fractional bookkeeper/CPA year 1)

## Per-team basketball staff (x16 teams: 8 boys, 8 girls)
- Head Coach (paid stipend)
- Assistant Coach (stipend)
- Interim/reserve coach pool (4-6 league-wide floaters, per-game stipend) — covers absences
- Team manager/ops volunteer or intern

## Game-day staff (seasonal)
- Certified officials (3-person crews at elite level)
- Certified athletic trainers (1-2 per venue session, required)
- Scorekeepers/clock operators
- Security + front gate
- Announcer/PA + DJ
- Stats crew (Synergy/Hudl-style tracking is a differentiator for recruiting)

## Media/production crew (seasonal)
- Producer/director
- Camera operators (2-4 cams)
- Commentary pairs (play-by-play + color; local media/college players)
- Social/content creators (highlight cutters, TikTok/IG reels — the Unrivaled growth engine)
- Photographer

## Volunteers/interns
- Belmont/Lipscomb/TSU sport management programs — internship pipeline
