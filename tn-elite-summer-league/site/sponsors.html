The Sponsor Universe

The full inventory — including the assets most local properties never think to sell. Pricing method: audience value ($0.10–$2.00/head) × visibility multiplier (jersey 2–4×, PA/banner 1.5×), with category exclusivity commanding a 2–5× premium. Streamed-game impressions are the upsell on every line. Ranges shown are Year 1; Full Vision reprices 2–4× higher.

How to package it

Don't sell 60 line items — sell 4 tiers: Title (1), Founding Partners (4–6 at $20–30K, category-exclusive, first-refusal on renewals forever), Official Partners (8–10 at $5–15K), and Game Night Partners ($1–5K). Every deal includes streamed-game impressions, social content, and a community story. Lead every pitch with the anti-AAU mission — sponsors aren't buying signage, they're buying the side of the story parents already agree with.
