The Feeling

Eighty of the best girls basketball players in Tennessee. Eighty of the best boys. One building. Every player earned the jersey through a tryout, a combine, and a draft — nobody bought a roster spot, so there are no passengers on the floor. When all ten players in a game are top-of-the-state talent, every possession is contested — the game plays at college speed because everyone on the floor forces it to. It's college basketball, arriving two years early. That's the product. Everything else is just how we deliver it.

The Format

Earned, Not Bought

The Draft
Tryout → Combine → 10-round draft
Statewide tryouts feed a combine, then 16 franchises draft ten rounds. Coaches never draft players from their own school.
The Week
Practices Mon & Tue · Games Thu & Sat
Sundays off, always. Boys practice Monday, girls Tuesday. Thursday League Night and Saturday Showcase on twin courts.
The Season
Two five-week seasons + playoffs
Season 1 and Season 2 blocks, each with playoffs, capped by a national invitational.
The Run
Buy-in team tournaments
Bracketed weekends for outside teams — hardware, banners and gear, never cash. In-building only; the broadcast stays the season's exclusive.

The Stage · Belmont University

Live From Belmont

The whole league lives under one roof in Nashville — Curb Event Center (5,085 seats) plus Belmont's 2021 twin-court practice facility. The pitch: Belmont as the Official Home of the League.

Officials
D2 college crews on every game
3-man crews for league games; 2-man for tournaments, 3-man for finals. Real college officiating — never AAU refs.
Broadcast
Real cameras, real crews
Manned broadcast cameras — no PTZ. Every league game streamed, with marquee games in full-crew production.
The Venue
Official Home of the League
Facilities in-kind as Belmont's sponsorship — higher-ed exclusivity, "Live from Belmont" on every broadcast, a Curb College production practicum.
