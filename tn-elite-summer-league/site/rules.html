Compliance, Handled

Clean By Design

Nothing about this league touches school-team rules — and we keep it that way with four hard guardrails.

Independent league TSSAA

Players sign up as individuals, outside their school programs — exactly like AAU, which runs all summer. TSSAA's independent-team restriction applies only during the school season. Dead-period rules restrict school coaches and their own players; our draft rule guarantees that pairing never exists. We'll still get written TSSAA confirmation on file — diligence, not a blocker.

Amateurism & NIL TSSAA

Players pay a (small) fee, keeping amateur status simple. Free-gear and expense questions get a written TSSAA ruling before launch. NIL is legal in TN with no cap — sponsor-to-player deals run direct, no school marks, no coach facilitation.

NCAA event certification ECAG

We certify as a nonscholastic event so D-I coaches can attend during live periods: BBCS registration for all athletes/coaches, USA Basketball Gold Licenses (~$68/coach, background check included), $1M/$2M liability with NCAA as additional insured, athletic trainer at every venue. Total fees are trivial (~$430 first year) — it's the process that must be airtight.

Safety stack

Background checks + SafeSport-style training for every adult. Certified athletic trainers at every event day. This is the "legit" families are starving for — and it's a selling point, not a cost center.

College-level officiating No AAU refs

The single loudest AAU complaint is terrible officiating — so we don't hire AAU crews, and we skip past HS crews too. Three-man college crews (D2) work every game, recruited through the college officiating coordinators we already know. Summer is their offseason; a full schedule of games at one venue is an easy yes. Budgeted at $150/official/game ($450/crew — ~$70K season) vs the $35–75 AAU pays. Players get taught the college whistle before they ever get to campus, and parents feel the difference in the first quarter.

Coach quality bar

Head coaches are recruited like a staff, not volunteers: college assistants, former pros, and decorated HS coaches (never with their own school's players). Every team also gets an assistant, and a league-wide interim pool guarantees no team ever goes uncoached. Real practices, real scouting, real film — development is the product.
