## 1. LOGLINE
A Nashville barista and single mom keeps postponing the wedding she and the man who became a father to her kids simply can't afford — until Dom and his three single coworkers surprise her with her dream gown, and in handing Sarah her forever, each of them edges closer to believing in their own.

---

## 2. STORY BREAKDOWN

**A-STORY — Dom's gift (the spine).** Dom set out to remove the one thing standing between Sarah and her wedding: the dress. He hand-picks three gowns, previews them on a model so the team can strategize, then surprises Sarah with a VIP fitting that ends in a "yes dress" and a party. The engine is *anticipation → reveal → celebration*; Dom's private stakes (a man who still believes in marriage after his own divorce) give the gift its weight.

**B-STORY — The team's own hearts.** All three women are single, pouring themselves into someone else's love story while quietly working on their own. Seeded at the rooftop (2f) and echoed in Danielle's self-love journey, it pays off at the party: helping Sarah say yes makes each of them want — and believe they deserve — their own person.

**C-STORY — Sarah & Garrison (the stakes).** They met at Sarah's brother's wedding. She's a single mom working a coffee counter; he's the steady guy who became a role model to her kids and writes her songs. They've set a date, changed it, postponed it — financial stress, life stress — always putting the wedding last. The dress is the first domino. Take it down and "it's happening now."

---

## 3. CHARACTER ARCS (start → want → obstacle → end)
- **Dom** — confident ringleader → wants to prove marriage is still worth believing in → his own divorce/lean years make it personal → undone by Sarah's tears, "balled like a baby," names the team his family.
- **Sarah** — guarded, "I don't know anything" → wants to feel like a bride, not a to-do list → a chaotic, money-tight life → "I feel like a wife." The dress beats her childhood picture.
- **Garrison** — the offscreen "why aren't you married yet?" → wants to finally give her the wedding → finances/timing/pride → set up to "ball his eyes out" at the aisle.
- **Danielle** — warm real-estate pro / dog mom → genuine connection after a rough history → patterns she's unlearning (visa marriage, a petty ex) → "I'm proud of myself," lands on gratitude.
- **Savannah** — the newcomer, freshly single → belonging and her own love story → still bruised → reframes what she's looking for: "someone that truly sees me in the little moments."
- **Muff** — skeptic-with-taste, "corset girly," here for work → not to get swept up → gets swept up → "I'm digging Sarah for sure."
