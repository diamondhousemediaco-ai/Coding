# Scene Prompt Template

Copy everything in the code block below into a new doc, paste your chosen
`Style:` block from `presets.md` at the top, then fill every `<…>`. Delete the
`*optional*` blocks you don't need. Keep the redundancy — the `Constraints`
recap at the bottom is supposed to restate the rules above.

Rule of thumb: if a rule keeps getting broken in generation, move it to
DIRECTOR'S NOTE #1 **and** repeat it in Constraints.

```
<PASTE A `Style:` BLOCK FROM presets.md HERE>

DIRECTOR'S NOTES — non-negotiable for every frame:

1. CHARACTER REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE — designs from <@[Image N] …> exactly 1-to-1,
   reference always overrides text.
2. ANATOMY LOCK — <subject> stays structurally solid and readable in every frame:
   one head, two arms ending in two hands, two legs, <exact prop count e.g. EXACTLY
   TWO revolvers one per hand — never three, never merging, never warping>. Clean
   separated silhouette. Nothing melts, smears, warps, or dissolves.
3. <MOTION LAW — the single most important thing this scene must get right, stated
   loud: e.g. "EVERYTHING IS FAST — she SPRINTS FLAT OUT at top speed the entire
   time, never jogging, no slow motion.">
4. <SPEED/ENERGY LIVES IN THE WORLD — what blasts past the lens, what explodes off
   the feet, what streams behind them.>
5. <WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR — e.g. "NO GIANT CREATURE, NO MONSTER of any kind." /
   "NO enemies, she is alone." Be explicit about the thing the engine tends to add.>
6. <LOOK-SPECIFIC LAW — Look A: LINELESS, no outlines. / Looks B & C: figures ON
   TWOS while atmosphere moves smoothly.>
7. <FACE / GEAR RENDER LAW — matte painted skin, eyes as simple shapes, props matte
   with no chrome/gloss, correctly sized, identical every frame.>
8. NO TEXT ANYWHERE — no letters, numbers, writing, or logos on any surface.

COMPOSITION LAW:
- <N> SHOTS WITH HARD CUTS — each short and urgent, a different size and angle.
- EVERY CAMERA MOVES <fast/…> — matched to the subject, never planted.
- SCREEN DIRECTION — <subject moves LEFT TO RIGHT>, camera stays on one side of the
  axis, never crossing the line.
- DEPTH STAGING — <near subject> / <mid action> / <deep background>.
- <Subject> stays LARGE and readable in every frame — never a small distant dot.

CHARACTER TAGS:
- <THE HERO> = the <…> from @[Image 2] — <full description baked in>.
- <THE PROP> = the <…> from @[Image 3], <held/worn how>.
- <THE WORLD> = the world of @[Image 1] — <location>.
- <STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: applying the studio/white reference background to the video.>

Cinematography: <Lubezki / Deakins>, physical cine lens — SHOT 1 a <24mm low
tracking …>, SHOT 2 a <35mm medium OTS …>, SHOT 3 an <85mm extreme close-up …>,
SHOT 4 a <16mm wide …>. Always moving, matched to the subject. <Handheld in human
hands / FPV drone> — <never tripod, dolly, crane, aerial>. Off-center framing,
rule of thirds + golden ratio; FORBIDDEN: centered or symmetric framing.

The 12 principles of animation: anticipation before every movement, squash and
stretch, follow-through and overlapping action on <cloth and hair>, arcs,
secondary action, exaggeration, and SOLID DRAWING from every angle.

Lighting: single motivated source — <flat overcast key / crisp morning sun /
moonlight> — character and world lit by the same light, flat painted shadow shapes,
no rim glow, no lens flare, no god rays, <no sun in frame>.

Emotional tone: <one line — the feeling>.

Color: 60:30:10 dominant <…> / secondary <…> / hot accent <…>.

Physics: gravity and inertia respected, real weight, correct contact shadows,
cause before effect. <Deep snow drags and explodes off her boots; recoil travels
up her arms; casings arc and fall.> Full real-time speed, nothing floats, nothing
morphs, <no slow motion>.

Audio: environmental SFX only — <list the specific sounds>. No music. No subtitles.

*VOICE ACTING DIRECTION (dialogue scenes only): <age, pitch, breath, emotional
undercurrent; how each key line differs>.
*LIP SYNC REQUIREMENT (dialogue scenes only): the speaking face is visible and the
mouth/lips/jaw form each syllable frame by frame, on twos, each phoneme a distinct
held pose <spell out mouth shapes for the language>. Lips drive the words. No
mumbling, no closed-mouth narration, no voice-over disconnected from the face.

SHOT 1 (0.0–<2.0>s) — <LENS + FRAMING + CAMERA MOVE>: <THE HERO from @[Image 2]
does X in THE WORLD of @[Image 1] …>.

HARD CUT to SHOT 2 (<2.0>–<4.0>s) — <LENS + FRAMING>: <action>.

HARD CUT to SHOT 3 (<4.0>–<5.5>s) — <LENS + FRAMING>: <action>.

HARD CUT to SHOT 4 (<5.5>–<8.0>s) — <LENS + FRAMING>: <action>.

Constraints: <restate every hard rule as one dense run-on — shot count and hard
cuts, matched screen direction, the motion law, the what-must-not-appear rule,
anatomy solid with exact prop count, look-specific law (lineless / on twos), props
matte no chrome, no text anywhere, atmosphere as painted clumps, designs from
@[Image 2]/@[Image 3] and world from @[Image 1] exactly> — no Pixar or DreamWorks
or anime face rendering, no photorealism, no 3D render, no CGI smoothness, matte
everything zero gloss no chrome, no lens flare, no god rays, no morphing no melting
no smearing no limb distortion no warping, no breath vapor, atmosphere painted
never volumetric, no music.
```

## Quick fill checklist

- [ ] Picked the right look (A gouache / B 2D-on-twos / C 2.5D-comic).
- [ ] Every `@Image N` has a CHARACTER TAG with the description baked in.
- [ ] The one thing the engine keeps getting wrong is DIRECTOR'S NOTE #1.
- [ ] Exact prop counts stated ("EXACTLY TWO …").
- [ ] A lens named for every shot; camera has a job in every shot.
- [ ] 60:30:10 color split written out.
- [ ] Audio SFX list is specific; "No music. No subtitles."
- [ ] Constraints run-on restates the rules and ends on the negative spine.
- [ ] Studio/white reference backgrounds forbidden.
- [ ] Generate several takes and cull.
