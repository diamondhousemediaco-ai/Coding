# Seedance video prompts — the Porsche film (3 shots per prompt)

The gouache house format for **video** (Seedance 2.0), 3 hard-cut shots per prompt.
Maps the 18 storyboard beats to **6 prompts × 3 shots**. Uses `@[Image N]` tags with
a per-prompt **upload key** — numbering resets to Image 1,2,3… with no gaps, order
**location → old man → car front → car rear → extra**. Both car angles ride together
whenever the car is on screen. Gauges are matte-painted with **blank ticks, no readable
numbers**. Front half (1–2) is calm; back half (3–6) is go-time. Generate several takes
and cull.

Ref library: **A** old man · **B** car front · **C** car rear (ducktail) · **D** garage
ext · **E** garage int · **F** desert plate · **G** desert road · **H** desert aerial.
