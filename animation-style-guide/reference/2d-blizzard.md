# Blizzard drama — hand-drawn 2D on twos (Look B)

Excerpted from the running "Higgsfield Prompts" collection. This is the look and
the **voice + lip-sync** discipline for dialogue drama. Verbatim (un-escaped).
Kazakh dialogue kept as written.

## The look (a fuller variant of the Look B style block)

Style: 8K IMAX, traditional hand-drawn 2D animation, animated on twos at 12 frames per second — each drawing held for two frames then replaced, choppy stepped motion cadence, hand-painted oil-brush texture, brushstrokes shifting and redrawn from frame to frame, line jitter, no 3D render, no game engine, no CGI smoothness.

CHARACTER REFERENCE IS THE ABSOLUTE SOURCE OF TRUTH. Replicate exact faces from @[Image 2](image_2) and @[Image 3](image_3) in every frame 1-to-1.

Character tags:

- THE MOTHER = woman from @[Image 2](image_2). Carrying tightly wrapped infant bundle.

- THE TODDLER = small girl from @[Image 3](image_3). Name Künekei.

- THE FOREST = location from @[Image 1](image_1).

Cinematography Lubezki / Deakins, fully handheld in human hands, anamorphic wide around 24mm, shallow depth, eye level or below, framing off-center with negative space, never tripod never dolly never crane never aerial.

The 12 principles of animation throughout: anticipation, squash and stretch, follow-through and overlapping, slow in slow out, arcs, secondary action, exaggeration, solid drawing.

Lighting: night, no light source, no moon, no stars, no rim, no contre-jour, no key light, no highlights. Flat dim diffuse ambient grey glow only, no direction. Flat painted shapes, no gradient shading.

Atmosphere: violent buran, hurricane gusts driving snow horizontally, visibility roughly 3 meters, beyond dissolves into white wall, rolling waves of denser blizzard sweeping the lens.

## Voice + lip-sync direction (the dialogue discipline)

VOICE ACTING DIRECTION — professional film acting, not flat narration.

THE TODDLER's voice: a real frightened three-year-old girl's voice, small, high, breathy, trembling, on the edge of crying but holding back, words coming out broken between shallow gasps, the first word stuck for a beat then released, child's voice not adult-imitating-child, never performed cute, just genuinely scared and exhausted, very thin from cold.

THE MOTHER's voice in this scene: started as hushed comfort, ends as urgent whisper-command. The comfort line is low, very close to the child's ear, breath audible between words, voice barely above the wind, the kind of voice a mother uses when she is herself terrified but cannot let the child hear that terror — controlled on the surface, vibrating underneath. The final whispered "жүгір" is different: voice stripped, urgent, almost no breath behind it, one word pushed out flat and direct, no comfort left.

LIP SYNC REQUIREMENT: when either character speaks, their face must be visible to the camera and their mouth, lips and jaw must clearly form the spoken Kazakh syllables frame by frame, animated on twos with each phoneme drawn as a distinct held pose — wide open mouth on "а" "о", lips rounding on "ұ" "ү" "ө", lips pressing together on "м" "п" "б", teeth showing on "т" "д" "с", small puff on "қ" "к". The toddler's mouth small and trembling between syllables. The mother's lips chapped and stiff from cold, opening reluctantly. Visible breath vapor with each spoken phrase. No mumbling, no closed-mouth narration, no voice-over disconnected from the face. Lips drive the words.

## Scene — the wolf howls

SCENE — medium handheld at chest height on THE MOTHER and THE TODDLER together in THE FOREST. THE MOTHER from @[Image 2](image_2) exact face and design, infant bundle pressed tight against chest. She has stopped walking, frozen, listening — wolf howls have just sounded from offscreen.

BEAT 1 (opening, no dialogue, ≈3s): THE MOTHER turns her head sharply on an arc, anticipation as her gaze sweeps the white-out — left, then right, then over her shoulder, slow in slow out on each turn. Eyes wide and scanning, jaw clenched, brow tense. She lowers herself into a protective crouch, pulls THE TODDLER hard against her hip with her free arm, presses the child's head into the side of her robe.

BEAT 2 (toddler speaks, ≈3s): THE TODDLER, exact face and design from @[Image 3](image_3), clings to her mother's leg, eyes wide and terrified, lip trembling visibly, small chest rising fast. She tilts her face up toward her mother so her own face is fully visible to the camera in profile-three-quarter. Her small mouth opens, the first word stuck for a fraction of a second behind her teeth, then released on a thin shaking breath, lips and jaw clearly forming each Kazakh syllable in distinct held poses on twos: "Апа..." She inhales sharply, lower lip quivering between words. "Қорқам." Another tiny inhale, voice breaking thinner. "Үйге барайық." Her eyes well on the last word but no tears fall.

BEAT 3 (mother answers, ≈4s): THE MOTHER bends down close to the child's ear, her face now in clear profile to the camera, one hand on the back of the child's cap. Her chapped lips part slowly, breath visible. She speaks low and close, lips and jaw moving on twos through each syllable, voice controlled on the surface and trembling underneath: "Тыныш..." A small swallow. "Құлыным." She kisses the top of the child's cap briefly. "Маған жақын жүр." A tiny inhale. "Се..."
