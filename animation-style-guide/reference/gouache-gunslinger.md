# Gunslinger — gouache look (Look A)

Two finished scenes from "Tim prompts". Verbatim (un-escaped).

## Scene 1 — Gunslinger vs. grunts, four shots

Style: 8K stylized animated feature film, hand-painted gouache animation — every frame a matte painted image with CLEAN ECONOMICAL RENDERING: simplified shapes, confident flat color planes, no over-detailing, painted like a production film still, not a detailed illustration. Completely matte, zero specular highlights, zero gloss, light diffusing like dry paint on paper, no 3D render gloss, no CGI smoothness, no Pixar or DreamWorks face rendering.

DIRECTOR'S NOTES — non-negotiable for every frame:

1. CHARACTER REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE — designs from @[Image 2](image_2) and @[Image 3](image_3) exactly 1-to-1, reference always overrides text.

2. EVERYTHING IS FAST — this is a TENSE, URGENT, HIGH-SPEED action scene. She SPRINTS FLAT OUT at absolute top speed the entire time — a fast driving stride rate, legs pumping hard, body pitched forward, covering ground quickly. She is never jogging, never loping, never at a comfortable pace. Every movement in the scene is quick and sharp. Nothing lingers, nothing drifts, nothing eases. No slow motion anywhere.

3. SPEED LIVES IN THE WORLD — the dark pine trunks BLAST past the lens with fast parallax, the snowy ground TEARS past beneath her, fog banks rip by. Snow explodes off her boots with every driving footfall. Her jacket and hair stream hard behind her in the airstream. The whole frame reads as velocity.

4. ANATOMY LOCK — her body stays structurally solid and readable in every frame: ONE head with a readable face, TWO distinct arms ending in TWO hands with correct fingers, EXACTLY TWO revolvers, one held in each hand — never three guns, never a third arm, never merging, never vanishing, never warping. Her silhouette stays clean and separated. Nothing melts, nothing smears. Every grunt likewise keeps one head, two arms and two legs, solid and separate.

5. WEAPON RENDER LAW — the revolvers are MATTE PAINTED props: flat painted metal, no chrome, no gloss, no product-render edges, no reflections. Compact and correctly proportioned to her hands, identical in shape in every frame.

6. MUZZLE FLASH IS GRAPHIC — every gunshot flash is a FLAT PAINTED GRAPHIC SHAPE, a bold hard-edged burst of orange and white paint — never a photographic glow, never a bloom, never a lens flare, never volumetric light.

7. THE GRUNTS ARE A REAL THREAT — the small soldiers from @[Image 3](image_3), every one of them matching that design exactly: bulky puffy off-white snow suits, dark visors, big bodies and small heads. They are PRESENT and CLOSE and MOVING FAST — sprinting through the trees, closing in from multiple directions, raising rifles, being hit and thrown backward with real force. Never distant silhouettes, never static, never vague shapes in fog. There are many of them, all identical to the reference, and they keep coming.

8. LINELESS PAINTING LAW — absolutely NO drawn lines anywhere: no outlines, no contour lines, no lineart, no dark eye rims. Every edge made only by one flat paint color meeting another, like a lineless gouache painting.

9. FACE RENDER LAW — two-tone matte painted skin, soft shadow roll-off, eyes as simple bold painted shapes, single-dot catchlights, no gradient CG shading, no gloss, no detail beyond the reference sheet.

10. NO GIANT CREATURE — there is no colossal beast, no giant, no monster, no large creature of any kind anywhere in this scene. Only the gunslinger and the small grunts.

11. NO TEXT ANYWHERE — no letters, no numbers, no writing, no logos, on any surface in any frame.

12. ATMOSPHERE — thick freezing fog between the pine trunks compressing the distance into flat pale white. Snow bursts and sprays as chunky painted clumps obeying real gravity, never volumetric simulation. No breath vapor, no smoke.

COMPOSITION LAW:

- FOUR SHOTS WITH HARD CUTS — fast cut-driven coverage, every shot a different size and angle, every shot short and urgent.

- EVERY CAMERA MOVES FAST — trucking, tracking or pushing, all matched to her sprint. Never planted, never locked-off, never slower than she is.

- SCREEN DIRECTION — she moves LEFT TO RIGHT, the camera staying on one side of the axis of action and never crossing the line.

- DEPTH STAGING — her in near frame, grunts in the middle distance closing in, the fogged treeline in deep space.

CHARACTER TAGS:

- THE GUNSLINGER = the woman from @[Image 2](image_2) — short choppy black hair with an undercut, cream shearling-collared jacket over a black t-shirt, black tactical pants, two compact silver revolvers.

- THE GRUNTS = the small soldiers from @[Image 3](image_3), exact design 1-to-1 — many of them, all matching the reference.

- THE FOREST = the world of @[Image 1](image_1) — a misty snowy pine forest.

Cinematography: physical cine lens — SHOT 1 a low 24mm tracking shot trucking with her at knee height at full sprint speed, SHOT 2 a 35mm medium over-the-shoulder whipping around as she fires, SHOT 3 an 85mm extreme close-up with a fast push, SHOT 4 a low 16mm wide tracking backward ahead of her as the grunts close in. Always moving, always fast.

The 12 principles of animation: hard anticipation before every movement, squash and stretch through her driving strides and the recoil, follow-through and overlapping action on her jacket and hair streaming behind her, arcs, secondary action, exaggeration, and SOLID DRAWING — every figure reads as a solid form with correct volume from every angle.

Lighting: single motivated key from a flat overcast sky, all characters and the forest lit by the same source, flat painted shadow shapes, no rim glow, no lens flare, no sun in frame.

Emotional tone: tense, urgent, dangerous — she is outnumbered and moving fast, lethal but pressured, the fight closing in around her.

Color: 60:30:10 dominant pale white snow and fog / dark pine trunks and grunt visors / hot accents of muzzle-flash orange and her cream jacket.

Physics: gravity and inertia respected, real weight, EXTREME speed — deep snow drags at her legs and explodes around her boots at every fast footfall, the revolvers kick with sharp recoil that travels up her arms, shell casings arc and fall with real gravity, grunts take hits and are thrown violently backward, all at full real-time speed with no slow motion. Nothing floats, nothing morphs.

Audio: environmental SFX only — hard fast crunching snow, ragged breathing, sharp gunshots, shell casings, grunt impacts. No music. No subtitles.

SHOT 1 (0.0–2.0s) — LOW TRACKING SHOT, 24mm, trucking alongside her at knee height at full sprint speed: THE GUNSLINGER from @[Image 2](image_2) is SPRINTING FLAT OUT from the very first frame through deep snow in the fogged forest of @[Image 1](image_1) — a fast driving stride rate, legs pumping hard, snow exploding around her boots in chunky painted clumps at every footfall, jacket and hair streaming hard behind her, revolvers held low. The dark pine trunks BLAST past the lens. Moving left to right. Behind her, grunts from @[Image 3](image_3) sprint into frame through the trees, chasing hard.

HARD CUT to SHOT 2 (2.0–4.0s) — MEDIUM OVER-THE-SHOULDER, 35mm, whipping around with her: still running flat out, she twists at the waist and FIRES both revolvers back over her shoulder — bold flat graphic bursts of orange and white paint erupting from both muzzles, her arms absorbing the sharp recoil, shell casings arcing away. Two grunts from @[Image 3](image_3) are hit and thrown violently backward through the fog, others keep sprinting in behind them, rifles raised.

HARD CUT to SHOT 3 (4.0–5.5s) — EXTREME CLOSE-UP, 85mm, fast push in on her face: her eyes fill the frame — bold flat painted shapes, hard and locked forward, hair whipping fast across her face, jaw set, breathing hard. She does not blink. One bloom of orange flash paints the fog behind her.

HARD CUT to SHOT 4 (5.5–8.0s) — LOW WIDE, 16mm, the camera tracking BACKWARD ahead of her at her sprint speed, facing back at her: she charges straight at the lens at full speed, both revolvers up and firing, muzzle flashes as flat graphic paint bursts — and behind her a swarm of grunts from @[Image 3](image_3) pours through the fogged trees from multiple directions, sprinting hard, some being hit and thrown down into the snow, the rest closing in. She never slows.

Constraints: four shots with hard cuts and matched screen direction left to right, every camera moving fast and matched to her sprint and never planted, SHE SPRINTS FLAT OUT AT FULL SPEED IN EVERY FRAME with a fast driving stride rate — never jogging, never loping, never a comfortable pace, everything already at full speed from the very first frame with no build-up and no standing, pine trunks BLASTING past with fast parallax and snow exploding at every footfall, NO SLOW MOTION anywhere, THE GRUNTS ARE PRESENT AND CLOSE AND MOVING FAST and every one of them matches @[Image 3](image_3) exactly — sprinting, raising rifles, being hit and thrown backward, never distant silhouettes and never static, NO GIANT CREATURE OR MONSTER OF ANY KIND ANYWHERE IN THIS SCENE, her anatomy solid in every frame with one head two arms two hands and EXACTLY TWO revolvers one per hand — never three guns, never merging, never warping, the revolvers matte painted with no chrome and no gloss and correctly sized to her hands, every muzzle flash a FLAT PAINTED GRAPHIC SHAPE and never a photographic glow or bloom or lens flare, no text no letters no numbers anywhere, snow bursts as chunky painted clumps never photoreal simulation, LINELESS painting with no outlines anywhere, designs from @[Image 2](image_2) and @[Image 3](image_3) and the world from @[Image 1](image_1) exactly, matte everything zero gloss, no breath vapor, no Pixar or DreamWorks or anime face rendering, no photorealism, no morphing no melting no limb distortion, no music.
