# Snowboarder — gouache look (Look A)

Two finished scenes from "Tim prompts". Verbatim (un-escaped). Note how Scene 2
is ONE continuous take (no cuts) while Scene 1 is three hard cuts — both in the
same look.

---

## Scene 1 — Freefall dive, three shots

Style: 8K stylized animated feature film, hand-painted gouache animation — every frame a matte painted image with CLEAN ECONOMICAL RENDERING: simplified shapes, confident flat color planes, no over-detailing, painted like a production film still, not a detailed illustration. Completely matte, zero specular highlights, zero gloss, light diffusing like dry paint on paper, no 3D render gloss, no CGI smoothness, no Pixar or DreamWorks face rendering.

DIRECTOR'S NOTES — non-negotiable for every frame:

1. CHARACTER REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE — face and design from @[Image 2](image_2) exactly 1-to-1, the head studies governing close framing, reference always overrides text.

2. LINELESS PAINTING LAW — absolutely NO drawn lines anywhere: no outlines, no contour lines, no lineart, no dark eye rims, no drawn facial lines, no seam lines. Every edge made only by one flat paint color meeting another, like a lineless gouache painting.

3. FACE RENDER LAW — two-tone matte painted skin, soft shadow roll-off, eyes as simple painted shapes behind the goggles, soft closed-mouth grin, never teeth, no gradient CG shading, no gloss.

4. GEAR RENDER LAW — board and goggles are matte painted props, no chrome, no product edges; THE BOARD stays burnt-orange in every single frame.

5. DIVE ORIENTATION — he falls in a real skydiver's dive: chest and face toward the earth, head low, arms swept back, knees bent so the burnt-orange board rides up behind his back. In the rolls he tucks tight, spins clean, and returns to the same face-down dive. He never stands upright in the air, never flails, never tumbles out of control — every roll is deliberate and athletic.

6. WIND IS VIOLENT AND NEVER PAUSES — hoodie and pant fabric rippling in fast waves, hood snapping, drawcords whipping, hair ripping back, everything obeying the freefall airstream in every frame. No breath vapor, no smoke.

COMPOSITION LAW:

- THREE SHOTS WITH HARD CUTS — each cut a different camera angle on the same continuous fall. The rider stays LARGE in every frame, at least a third of frame height.

- COVERED BACKGROUNDS — the cobalt sky, cloud sea and painted peaks of @[Image 1](image_1) fill every frame around him; the mountain face grows closer across the three cuts.

CHARACTER TAGS:

- THE RIDER = the snowboarder from @[Image 2](image_2), exact design head to toe, goggles down over his eyes. THE BOARD = the burnt-orange snowboard from @[Image 3](image_3), strapped to his boots, riding up behind him in the dive. THE MOUNTAIN = the world of @[Image 1](image_1), below him and rushing closer.

Cinematography: FPV drone in freefall, wide lens, smooth tracking flight matched to his fall rate — steady tracking on cut one, freer orbiting moves on cuts two and three, every move continuous within its shot.

The 12 principles of animation: arcs on every camera path, every tuck and every roll, follow-through and overlapping action on fabric and hair through the spins, secondary action, solid drawing at every angle.

Lighting: single unified source — crisp morning sun, rider and world lit by the same light, flat painted shadow shapes, no rim glow, no lens flare.

Emotional tone: euphoric calm — the fall of his life, cocky and loose, playing in the air.

Color: 60:30:10 dominant white cloud sea and peaks / cobalt sky / hot accents of his outfit and the burnt-orange board.

Physics: gravity and inertia respected — he accelerates through the dive, rolls carry real rotational momentum and resolve cleanly back into the dive, fabric and hair rip with real airflow, the camera falls under the same physics, no floating, no slow motion anywhere.

Audio: environmental SFX only — roaring freefall wind, fabric flapping hard, the pitch shifting as he rolls. No music. No subtitles.

SHOT 1 (0.0–3.0s) — WIDE POV TRACKING FROM BELOW: the camera falls directly beneath THE RIDER from @[Image 2](image_2), looking straight up at him as he DIVE-BOMBS toward the lens — face-down, head low, his painted face clearly readable behind the goggles, soft closed-mouth grin, arms swept back, the burnt-orange board from @[Image 3](image_3) up behind his back — the huge cobalt sky and drifting clouds of @[Image 1](image_1) filling the frame behind him. The camera holds a steady distance below, tracking his fall, his jacket rippling hard, the wide framing showing his whole body small-to-large against the vast sky.

HARD CUT to SHOT 2 (3.0–5.5s) — SIDE ORBIT: the camera falls level with him and arcs around his side as he TUCKS — knees pulling up, arms wrapping in — and rips a clean lateral air roll, the painted world wheeling behind him, fabric whipping around the spin with follow-through, then he snaps back out into the face-down dive, grinning.

HARD CUT to SHOT 3 (5.5–8.0s) — HIGH THREE-QUARTER, LOOKING DOWN PAST HIM: the camera falls just above and behind his shoulder, the vast painted face of THE MOUNTAIN filling the frame below — he tucks again and rolls once more, the burnt-orange board flashing through the rotation, then stretches back into the dive as the cloud sea rushes up to meet him, the peaks growing large around the edges of frame.

Constraints: three distinct shots with hard cuts, the rider face-down chest-to-earth in every non-roll moment — never upright, never standing in the air — every roll deliberate tucked and clean with real rotational momentum, the rider large in every frame, his face clearly visible and readable in shot one, real gravity and acceleration throughout, no slow motion, LINELESS painting with no outlines anywhere, design from @[Image 2](image_2) board from @[Image 3](image_3) world from @[Image 1](image_1) exactly, soft closed-mouth grin never teeth, no breath vapor, matte everything with zero gloss, no Pixar or DreamWorks or anime rendering, no photorealism, no lens flare, no morphing no melting no limb distortion, no music.

## Scene 2 — Helicopter exit, one continuous handheld take

Style: 8K stylized animated feature film, hand-painted gouache animation — every frame a matte painted image with CLEAN ECONOMICAL RENDERING: simplified shapes, confident flat color planes, no over-detailing, painted like a production film still, not a detailed illustration. Completely matte, zero specular highlights, zero gloss, light diffusing like dry paint on paper, no 3D render gloss, no CGI smoothness, no Pixar or DreamWorks face rendering.

DIRECTOR'S NOTES — non-negotiable for every frame:

1. CHARACTER REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE — face and design from @[Image 2](image_2) exactly 1-to-1, the head studies on the sheet governing all close framing, reference always overrides text.

2. LINELESS PAINTING LAW — absolutely NO drawn lines anywhere: no outlines, no contour lines, no lineart, no dark rims around the eyes, no drawn nose or lip lines, no inked brows, no seam lines on gloves or clothing. Every edge is made only by one flat paint color meeting another, like a lineless gouache painting.

3. FACE RENDER LAW — two-tone matte painted skin, soft shadow roll-off, clean minimal strokes, eyes as simple painted shapes with single-dot catchlights, no gradient CG shading, no gloss, no added detail beyond the reference sheet. His expression is always a SOFT CLOSED-MOUTH GRIN — a small easy smirk, lips together, never a full smile, never teeth.

4. GEAR AND CABIN RENDER LAW — goggles, bindings and the cabin are matte painted surfaces exactly like @[Image 4](image_4), no chrome, no product edges, no screens; goggle lenses reflect the peaks only as flat painted marks.

5. FAST CASUAL GESTURES — every action quick and offhand, athlete-casual, never slow, never ceremonial: the thumbs-up flashes for barely a second, the goggle pull is one snap, the exit is sudden. Real-time speed, no slow motion, no lingering.

6. ROTOR WASH IS VIOLENT AND NEVER PAUSES — hair thrashing hard, hood snapping, drawcords whipping horizontal in every frame. No breath vapor, no smoke, no mist from his mouth at any point.

COMPOSITION LAW:

- ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT — a single unbroken handheld take, NO cuts, no editing. The camera is a FILMER WHO JUMPS: he starts crouched in the cabin with the rider, moves close for the goggle snap, and then LEAPS OUT OF THE HELICOPTER WITH HIM, falling alongside him the rest of the shot. Handheld action-cam energy throughout — alive, breathing, reactive — never tripod-static, never violent shake.

- COVERED BACKGROUNDS — inside the cabin the shot faces ACROSS the cabin of @[Image 4](image_4) toward the open door with the peaks and cloud sea of @[Image 1](image_1) burning bright behind him; once airborne, the vast painted face and peaks of @[Image 1](image_1) fill every frame.

CHARACTER TAGS:

- THE RIDER = the snowboarder from @[Image 2](image_2), exact design head to toe. THE BOARD = the burnt-orange snowboard from @[Image 3](image_3), strapped to his boots throughout. THE CABIN = the interior from @[Image 4](image_4), nothing invented. THE MOUNTAIN = the world of @[Image 1](image_1).

Cinematography: physical cine lens, wide, handheld filmer — cabin crouch, push-in, then freefall tracking, eye level with the rider at all times.

The 12 principles of animation: anticipation before every gesture and before the drop, squash and stretch, follow-through and overlapping action on hair and fabric lagging the body, fast snappy timing, arcs, secondary action, solid drawing.

Lighting: single unified source — crisp morning sun, character, cabin and sky lit by the same light, flat painted shadow shapes, no rim glow, no lens flare.

Emotional tone: cocky pre-drop electricity — a small knowing smirk, zero hesitation, the exit as casual as stepping off a curb, then pure joy in the fall.

Color: 60:30:10 dominant white peaks and cloud sea / charcoal cabin and cobalt sky / hot accents of his outfit and the burnt-orange board.

Physics: gravity and inertia respected, real weight, everything loose obeying the violent downdraft, cause before effect — when he pushes off he DROPS fast and heavy, board first, hair and hoodie ripping upward as he accelerates; the camera falls at the same rate, locked to him.

Audio: environmental SFX only — roaring rotor, wind hammering the door, fabric snapping, then the rotor swallowed by roaring freefall wind. No music. No subtitles.

THE SHOT — one continuous 8-second handheld take:

(0.0–1.5s) The camera drifts toward THE RIDER from @[Image 2](image_2) sitting in the roaring open doorway against the blazing peaks and cloud sea of @[Image 1](image_1), THE BOARD from @[Image 3](image_3) on his boots. He looks over with a soft closed-mouth grin and flashes a fast thumbs-up, hair thrashing.

(1.5–3.0s) The camera PUSHES IN CLOSE — his face filling the frame, matching the head studies of @[Image 2](image_2) — as he snaps the goggles down from forehead to eyes in one quick motion, anticipation, snap, settle, the peaks reflecting in the lenses as flat painted marks, his smirk sharpening.

(3.0–4.5s) He plants his hands on the sill and swings the board out into the wind, a quick anticipation crouch — the camera pulling back just enough to hold him full in frame, leaning out over the void with him.

(4.5–8.0s) He pushes off and DROPS — and the camera JUMPS WITH HIM, plunging out of the helicopter into freefall, tracking him from just above and behind as he falls fast and heavy toward the vast painted face far below, board first, jacket and hair ripping upward, the cloud sea rushing up, the helicopter shrinking away above the top of frame. The camera stays locked on him the whole fall, buffeted by the wind, the world of @[Image 1](image_1) filling every frame.

Constraints: ONE continuous shot with no cuts, the camera physically exits the helicopter with the rider and freefalls alongside him, all gestures fast and casual with real-time speed and no slow motion, soft closed-mouth grin only — never a full smile, never teeth — LINELESS painting with no outlines no contour lines no eye rims no drawn details anywhere, no breath vapor no smoke, design from @[Image 2](image_2) board from @[Image 3](image_3) cabin from @[Image 4](image_4) world from @[Image 1](image_1) exactly, faces matte and clean with no gradient shading no gloss and no detail beyond the reference, handheld filmer camera never tripod-static never violently shaky, real gravity on the drop — both rider and camera fall fast and heavy, no floating, no Pixar or DreamWorks or anime rendering, no photorealism, no lens flare, no morphing no melting no limb distortion, no music.
