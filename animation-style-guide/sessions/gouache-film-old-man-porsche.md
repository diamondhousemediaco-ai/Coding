## 1. The House Style

Every image/video in this world is **hand-painted gouache animation**. The reusable
style block:

```
Style: 8K stylized animated feature film, hand-painted gouache animation — every frame a
matte painted image with CLEAN ECONOMICAL RENDERING: simplified shapes, confident flat
color planes, no over-detailing, painted like a production film still, not a detailed
illustration. Completely matte, zero specular highlights, zero gloss, light diffusing like
dry paint on paper, no 3D render gloss, no CGI smoothness, no Pixar or DreamWorks face
rendering.
```

**The non-negotiable laws (used across every prompt):**
- **LINELESS PAINTING** — no outlines, no contour lines, no lineart, no dark eye rims;
  every edge is one flat paint color meeting another.
- **FACE RENDER** — two-tone matte skin, soft shadow roll-off, eyes as simple bold
  painted shapes with single-dot catchlights, no gradient CG shading, no gloss.
- **ANATOMY LOCK** — figures stay solid and readable: one head, two arms, two hands with
  correct fingers, two legs; nothing merges, melts, warps or distorts.
- **REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE** — attached character/object/world references match 1-to-1;
  reference always overrides text.
- **NO TEXT ANYWHERE** — no letters, numbers, logos or signage on any surface.
- **MATTE EVERYTHING** — no gloss on paint, chrome, glass or metal.
- **Color 60:30:10.** Physics respected — real weight, real gravity, no floating, no
  morphing. Audio: environmental SFX only, no music.

## 2. The City Hero

Concept: a normal cool young adult flowing through a big city with **ice / "Frozone"
powers** (Incredibles-style). Grounded street-level, hard sunlight on concrete.

Key added law for this world — the **ICE RENDER LAW** (because AI defaults ice to glossy
glass): all ice is flat matte painted — cyan/white paint planes with hard facets, no
glass, no refraction, no reflections, no sparkle, no glow, no bloom.

### City flow video prompt (4 shots, hard cuts)

```
Style: 8K stylized animated feature film, hand-painted gouache animation — matte painted,
clean economical rendering, flat color planes, zero gloss, no Pixar/DreamWorks face
rendering.
DIRECTOR'S NOTES:
1. CHARACTER REFERENCE IS ABSOLUTE — design/face from @[Image 2] 1-to-1.
2. HE IS A NORMAL YOUNG ADULT — everyday streetwear, no costume/cape/mask/emblem/glowing
   eyes/powered armor.
3. ICE RENDER LAW — all ice flat matte painted, cyan and white paint planes with hard
   facets; NO glass, refraction, transparency, reflections, sparkle, glow, bloom or shine.
4. ICE PHYSICS LAW — ice is SOLID and obeys gravity: a ribbon races out ahead of his
   hands/feet with real thickness, ramps grow off the street with weight, and behind him it
   cracks into CHUNKY PAINTED SHARDS that fall/bounce/skitter. Never floats, never mist.
5. NO POWER GLOW — no energy beams, magic, particles, swirls, light rays, VFX aura. The
   painted ice is the only effect.
6. EVERYTHING IS FAST — full skating speed from frame one, carving hard, no slow motion.
7. SPEED LIVES IN THE WORLD — buildings, cars, poles, pedestrians blast past with fast
   parallax, ice chips spray at every carve, jacket/hair stream behind.
8. ANATOMY LOCK — one head, two arms/hands, two legs/shoes; clean separated silhouette.
9. LINELESS PAINTING LAW — no outlines anywhere.
10. FACE RENDER LAW — two-tone matte skin, bold flat eyes, single-dot catchlights, a soft
    closed-mouth grin, never teeth.
11. NO TEXT ANYWHERE — all city signage is blank painted color panels.
12. ATMOSPHERE — crisp cold snap, pale frost blooms on asphalt, ice chips as chunky
    painted clumps, no breath vapor/steam/smoke.
COMPOSITION: four shots, hard cuts, every camera moving fast, screen direction left to
right, he stays large in frame, depth staging near/mid/deep.
CHARACTER TAGS: THE SKATER = @[Image 2] (everyday streetwear, no costume). THE ICE = pale
cyan painted ice (ribbon, ramps, shards). THE CITY = @[Image 1].
Cinematography: SHOT 1 low 24mm trucking at knee height; SHOT 2 35mm orbit as he launches;
SHOT 3 85mm extreme close-up fast push; SHOT 4 low 16mm wide tracking backward down the
avenue. Always moving, always fast.
Lighting: single low crisp winter sun, long painted shadows, no rim glow, no lens flare,
no sun in frame.
Emotional tone: effortless joy and cocky flow — pure play, he owns the city.
Color: 60:30:10 warm sunlit concrete / cool shadow and asphalt / pale cyan ice and jacket.
SHOT 1 (0.0–2.0s) — LOW TRACKING 24mm: he CARVES into frame down a sunlit avenue on a
ribbon of pale cyan ice racing ahead, one hand trailing to feed it, leaning hard, ice
chips spraying, ribbon cracking to shards behind, cars/poles blasting past, left to right.
HARD CUT SHOT 2 (2.0–4.0s) — 35mm ORBIT: he throws a hand forward, a RAMP of painted ice
erupts up, he LAUNCHES over a moving bus, body extended, skyline wheeling, a fresh ribbon
already racing out below.
HARD CUT SHOT 3 (4.0–5.5s) — 85mm ECU fast push on his face: eyes locked forward, hair
ripping back, a calm closed-mouth grin, sunlit buildings streaking behind.
HARD CUT SHOT 4 (5.5–8.0s) — LOW 16mm WIDE tracking backward: he SLAMS onto a fresh
ribbon, drops into a deep crouch and carves at the lens, one hand skimming the ice, the
ribbon shattering behind, traffic blasting past, skyline deep. Never slows.
Constraints: matte flat painted ice with no glass/refraction/reflection/sparkle/glow/bloom,
ice solid and gravity-bound breaking into chunky shards, no energy/particles/VFX,
everyday streetwear no costume, anatomy solid, no text/signage/logos, LINELESS no outlines,
matte zero gloss, soft closed-mouth grin never teeth, no breath vapor, no Pixar/DreamWorks/
anime rendering, no photorealism, no lens flare, no morphing/melting, no music.
```

**Direction choices made:** crisp low winter sun (daytime), pure play (no chase), street-
level ice ribbon he rides, ends carving at the lens.

## 4. The Car

**Canon:** a **1978-era white narrow-body Porsche 911 SC** — G-series impact-bumper car,
black accordion bumpers, round headlights, **Fuchs** five-spoke wheels, and a small
**body-colored ducktail spoiler**.

### Ducktail research (the spoiler ID)
On a narrow-body SC there are three factory tails: the **ducktail (Entenbürzel)** — small,
*body-colored* molded lip; the **whale tail** — larger flat tray with a black rubber
surround; and the **tea tray** — large fiberglass enclosure. The car in the reference is
the **ducktail** (compact, painted white to match the body).
Sources: FCP Euro spoiler guide; Rennlist "Whale Tail, Duck Tail, or No Tail?"; Porsche.com
"most iconic Porsche spoilers."

## 7. The Short Film

**Premise:** an old man fixes his old Porsche in his desert garage — under the car, then
he takes it out and drives it on the red desert roads. Arc: **dawn → the work → pride →
firing it up → the release.**

### Rules
- **Both car angles (front + rear) ride together any time the car is on screen.**
- **Per-prompt image numbering resets to Image 1, 2, 3… with no gaps** (a prompt can't say
  "Image 1 and Image 5" — there must be five images for it to be Image 5). Order:
  **location → old man → car front → car rear**. Each prompt gets an **upload key**.

### Blocking — 5 generations × 3 cuts (condensed from 14 shots)

**GEN 1 — "DAWN / THE GARAGE"** · EXT, morning · refs: garage ext, desert
- wide: lone garage in red desert at dawn → ground level: front, open bay, cacti → slow
  push toward the dark glowing doorway

**GEN 2 — "THE WORK"** · INT, morning · refs: garage int, old man, car front, car rear
- wide: car on jack stands, boots sticking out → close: hands on the wrench under the car
  → extreme close: his face under the car

**GEN 3 — "PRIDE"** · INT, morning · refs: garage int, old man, car front, car rear
- rolls out on the creeper, sits up → stands, wipes hands, appraises, keys in hand → close:
  lowers the engine deck, hand on the ducktail

**GEN 4 — "IGNITION / ROLL-OUT"** · INT→EXT, day · refs: garage int, old man, car front,
car rear, desert
- close: turns the key, engine fires, exhaust puff → car noses out toward flooding daylight
  → EXT: pulls onto the red sand, swings toward the road

**GEN 5 — "THE DRIVE"** · EXT, golden hour · refs: car front, car rear, desert road,
desert aerial, old man
- low tracking: tears down the black road → aerial: tiny car carving through vast desert →
  close: old man at the wheel, golden light, faint smile *(button)*

> GEN 4 and GEN 5 need 5 references (others are 2–4). If the tool gets shaky at 5, split
