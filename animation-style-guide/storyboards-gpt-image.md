# Storyboards — GPT Image 2 (6-panel sheets)

The amped **18-panel** version of the Porsche film for GPT Image 2. Board 1 keeps
the calm ritual; Boards 2–3 are "go time" — low hero angles, close-ups on the tach,
shifter, and gas pedal, exploding dust, dutch tilts.

**GPT Image notes:** reads plain prose + **attached references** (no `@Image` tags),
one still, and mangles text — so gauges are painted with **blank ticks, no readable
numbers** (keeps the no-text law). Attach the refs listed above each prompt.
**Orientation: 16:9.** GPT Image's native landscape cap is 3:2 (1536×1024); if it
won't produce true 16:9, generate at the widest it allows — the panels still read
widescreen — or crop the sheet to 16:9.

Ref library: **A** old man · **B** car front · **C** car rear (ducktail) · **D** garage
ext · **E** garage int · **F** desert plate · **G** desert road · **H** desert aerial.

## Amped blocking (3 boards × 6 panels)

**BOARD 1 — THE RITUAL (calm):** garage at dawn → car on stands, boots out → hands on
the wrench → rolls out, wipes hands → stands, appraises, keys in hand → hand on the

**BOARD 2 — GO TIME: IGNITION & LAUNCH (high energy):** key twist → tach needle snaps up

**BOARD 3 — GO TIME: FULL SEND (high energy):** tears down the black road → speedo climbing
→ hands working the wheel → aerial carve → powerslide through a bend → old man at the
wheel, golden light, faint smile *(button)*.

## The 6-reference caveat (Board 1)

~5. The desert plate only appears in Panel 1, so if a generation comes out muddy:
drop the desert plate back to prose for that sheet ("a vast open red-sand desert with
faint low mesas on the far horizon"), or split Panel 1 off as its own exterior
generation. The interior five (garage int, old man, both car angles) must stay
attached.
