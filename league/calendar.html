Summer 2027

The Calendar

Because the league is fully independent of school programs — and no coach is ever paired with his or her own high-school players — the TSSAA dead period does not stop league play. Same rulebook AAU operates under all summer. We still design marquee weekends around NCAA live periods so college coaches can be in the building.

March – April 2027
Statewide Tryouts

Regional tryout stops (Memphis, Knoxville, Chattanooga, Tri-Cities, Nashville). ~600 athletes.

Apr 30 – May 1, 2027
The Combine — Belmont

Two days, wrapped before AP exams begin May 3. Testing stations (each one sellable — "Vertical Jump powered by ___"), scrimmages, interviews, media stations.

Fri May 14, 2027
Draft Night — Curb Event Center

Deliberately placed: the evening the AP window closes, a week before graduation weekends peak. 16 teams, live-streamed, sponsor-branded green room, draft caps. Rule: no player may be drafted onto a team coached by anyone from their own school.

Jun 1 – Jul 3
Season 1 5 weeks

8 regular-season games per team + week-5 playoffs. Championship Saturday in the arena.

Jul 6 – Aug 7
Season 2 5 weeks

Fresh seed, same rosters (or a mid-summer trade window — a content goldmine). Marquee weekends Jul 8–11 & Jul 15–18 aligned to NCAA men's live periods for max college-coach attendance.

Year 2–3 · Mid July
The Nationals Invitational 10 days

16 invited programs nationally (8 boys / 8 girls), placed inside the live-period window. Entry fees + host-hotel economics fund it.
