The gap between revenue and cost is not a problem; it's the size of the raise. (Unrivaled raised $35M before its first game and nearly broke even in season one.)

The Belmont play — turn the landlord into a sponsor

Before signing a rental, pitch Belmont as "Official Home of the League" — facilities in-kind as their founding sponsorship (~$74K of venue cost at market rates). What they get: exclusive higher-ed category, "Live from Belmont University" on 150+ broadcasts, their court in every highlight, an admissions booth in Vendor Row in front of 600 tryout athletes and their college-shopping families all summer, and summer production practicum for Curb College media students. Even a hybrid (half rent + student production support) swings this budget $35–50K. Offer the menu — full in-kind, hybrid, or straight rental — and sign date holds under whichever they'll do fastest.

Full editable model in the interactive budget page and Excel workbook. Biggest unknowns needing real quotes: Belmont rental (no published rates — Belmont Strategic Venues), production vendor bids, and title-sponsor appetite.
