The Million-Dollar Playbook

Stop aiming at $500K. Stack the sources below and $1M is a plan, not a stretch — the conservative column alone clears ~$975K, and it changes what Year 1 looks like: arena nights every week, owned broadcast rig, a real NIL pool, zero corners cut. Sequenced right, each wave sells the next.

Plus the invisible line: in-kind

Host hotel rooms, dealership vehicles, team meals, printing, and production trade don't show up as cash — but $100K+ of budget relief hits the bottom line exactly the same. Every in-kind deal you close makes the cash stack go further. As a media company, Diamond House can trade content production for almost anything.

Sequencing is the whole game

Wave 1: Founding Team Owners — scarcity sells itself and gives you ~$300K of social proof. Wave 2: title + founding partners, priced off that momentum ("the owners are already in"). Wave 3: the equity round, pitched with $500K+ of commitments as traction. Wave 4: community round + memberships at brand launch, when hype peaks. Each wave is the proof for the next one.
