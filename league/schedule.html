Every Game Counts

Mock Season Schedule

This is what a season looks like on the ground. Eight franchise brands, each fielding a boys and a girls team — one identity, two divisions, double the fanbase. Franchise names below are mock placeholders (final brands TBD): Forge · Delta · Ridge · Rhythm · Crush · Tri-Star · Summit · Gold Rush. The rhythm is the college rhythm: practices Monday & Tuesday, games Thursday & Saturday, Sundays off — always. Full date-by-date detail lives in the Master Schedule page.

Mon & Tue · Practice Nights

Boys Monday, girls Tuesday — full-court 90-minute slots on the twin courts, rotating weekly. Wednesday film/lift optional. Real practice-to-game ratio, the thing AAU never gives.

Thursday · League Night

All 16 teams play — 8 games on two courts, tips 4:45 / 6:15 / 7:45 / 9:15. Every game streamed with commentary.

Saturday · Showcase Saturday

All 16 teams again — franchise doubleheaders at 12:00 & 1:45, then the marquee evening block (3:30 / 5:15 / 7:00 / 8:45) with full broadcast crews. Sunday: off, always.

Seeding · how stakes stay real
Seeds 1–8 by record. Tiebreaks: head-to-head, then point differential capped at ±15 per game — so nobody runs up a score on kids, but every possession still matters.

The Summer Cup — closing night, Aug 7

If Season 1 and Season 2 crown different champions, they meet in a winner-take-all Champions Game to close the summer. If one team sweeps both seasons, they hang a "Perfect Summer" banner in the arena — and chase history gives every Season 2 game a storyline. Either way, the last night of the summer always has the biggest stakes.
