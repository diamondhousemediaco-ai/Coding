# NASHVILLE BRIDE — Story Document / Paper Edit
## Episode: "The Yes Dress" (Sarah & Garrison)

---

## 1. LOGLINE
A Nashville barista and single mom keeps postponing the wedding she and the man who became a father to her kids simply can't afford — until Dom and his three single coworkers surprise her with her dream gown, and in handing Sarah her forever, each of them edges closer to believing in their own.

---

## 2. STORY BREAKDOWN

**A-STORY — Dom's gift (the spine).**
Dom set out to remove the one thing standing between Sarah and her wedding: the dress. He hand-picks three gowns, previews them on a model so the team can strategize, then surprises Sarah with a VIP fitting that ends in a "yes dress" and a party. The engine of the episode is *anticipation → reveal → celebration*, and Dom's private stakes — a man who still believes in marriage after his own divorce — give the gift its weight.

**B-STORY — The team's own hearts (personal investment).**
All three women are single, pouring themselves into someone else's love story while quietly working on their own. Seeded at the rooftop in **Savannah's emotional story (2f)** and echoed in Danielle's self-love / "shadow work" journey, it pays off at the party: helping Sarah say yes makes each of them want — and believe they deserve — their own person. "We'll find our people one day."

**C-STORY — Sarah & Garrison (the stakes).**
They met at Sarah's brother's wedding, both in the wedding party. She's a single mom working a coffee counter; he's the steady guy who became a role model to her kids and writes her songs. They've set a date, changed it, and postponed it — financial stress, life stress — always putting the wedding last. The dress is the first domino. Take it down and "it's happening now."

---

## 3. CHARACTER ARCS (start → want → obstacle → end)

- **Dom** — *Start:* generous ringleader, all confidence and plan. *Wants:* to give Sarah a moment that proves marriage is still worth believing in. *Obstacle:* his own history — divorce, lean years, the thing he "had to give up" — makes this personal in a way he doesn't expect. *End:* undone by Sarah's tears, "balled like a baby," names the team his family.
- **Sarah (bride)** — *Start:* guarded, out of her element, "I don't know anything" about the day. *Wants:* to feel like a bride instead of a to-do list. *Obstacle:* a chaotic, money-tight life that's made the wedding feel impossible. *End:* "I feel like a wife." Glowing, crying, sure — the dress is better than what she pictured as a little girl.
- **Garrison (partner)** — *Start:* offscreen, the quiet "why aren't you married yet?" question. *Wants:* to finally give Sarah the wedding she deserves. *Obstacle:* finances, timing, pride. *End (offscreen but felt):* set up to "ball his eyes out" at the aisle; Dom frames him as the good man who earned this.
- **Danielle** — *Start:* warm, detail-loving real-estate pro and dog mom (Grace & Frankie). *Wants:* genuine connection after a rough romantic history. *Obstacle:* old patterns she's actively unlearning ("shadow work," a marriage that was really a visa, a petty ex). *End:* "I'm proud of myself" — pours her light into Sarah, lands on gratitude.
- **Savannah** — *Start:* the newcomer (PA → Maryland → Nashville), bubbly, still finding her footing. *Wants:* belonging and her own love story. *Obstacle:* she's the most freshly single, still bruised. *End (B-story payoff):* seeing Sarah and Garrison reframes what she's looking for — "someone that truly sees me in the little moments."
- **Muff** — *Start:* the skeptic-with-taste, "corset girly," here for work, guarding her excitement. *Wants:* to nail the dress details and not get swept up. *Obstacle:* she gets swept up anyway. *End:* "I'm digging Sarah for sure" — predicts the yes dress, melts at the reveal, "bridal superheroes."
